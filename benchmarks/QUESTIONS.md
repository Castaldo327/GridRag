# Frozen analyst question matrix

These 30 questions and reference queries were fixed before the live comparison. References live in `benchmarks/cases.py` and never reach either pipeline. Difficulty describes analytical work, not observed success.

| # | Difficulty | Area | Analyst question | What it tests |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 2 | easy | scope | What was Texas's peak actual hourly electricity demand on October 20, 2026, local time, in MW? | Regional entity and actual demand |
| 3 | easy | products | How much solar power was California generating at 12:05 pm local time on October 18, 2026, in MW? | Native five-minute generation observation |
| 4 | easy | joins | How many active solar projects are in New York's current interconnection queue? | Proposed projects versus operational output |

| 6 | easy | products | What was the real-time LMP at NYISO_BUS_B at 5:10 pm local time on October 18, 2026, in USD/MWh? | Individual bus and total LMP |
| 7 | easy | operations | How much resource capacity was unavailable in MISO on October 18, 2026, counting both planned and forced outages, in MW? | Daily unavailable capacity versus energy |
| 8 | medium | joins | What percentage of PJM's total net generation came from solar during August 2026? | Implicit denominator and protection against join fanout |

| 24 | very hard | temporal | For New York's delivery hours on October 19, 2026 local time, what was the mean absolute load-forecast error in MW using only forecasts known by October 19 at 00:00 UTC? | 25 | very hard | operations | What was the load-weighted average real-time ERCOT_HUB LMP on October 21, 2026 local time, in USD/MWh? Weight each five-minute price by actual five-minute load in the same interval. | Weighted measure and cadence alignment |
| 26 | very hard | operations | On October 20, 2026 local time, which California hour had the highest actual hourly demand minus hourly wind and solar generation? | 28 | very hard | regional | Across all seven ISOs, what was the mean absolute hourly load-forecast error for delivery on October 18, 2026 in each ISO's local time, in MW? Return all 21 combinations as iso_code, product, average_energy_price in USD/MWh, using product labels real_time_bus, real_time_hub, and day_ahead_hub. | Wide result completeness and price-component discrimination |
